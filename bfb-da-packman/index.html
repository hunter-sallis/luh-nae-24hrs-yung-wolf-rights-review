How to read these statuses
  • No MLC work identified: no matching work found in the scope reviewed; investigate before filing a new registration.
  • Registered · shares remain open: a work exists with less than 100% total known shares; the remainder is not the artist’s personal split.
  • Registered · 100% claimed: the work is fully claimed across listed interests; artist attribution still requires a separate review.
  • Writer attribution needs verification: a matching work exists, but the artist’s legal writer identity is not established on its writer line.
  • Work found · recording link unconfirmed: title and legal writer match an existing work, but the reviewed recording link was not confirmed.
  • Recording-to-work match needs review: an attached work title differs; check audio, versions and agreements before any correction.
  • Needs verification: a possible composition match or other unresolved evidence prevents a reliable classification.
