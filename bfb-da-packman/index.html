  • Recording-to-work match needs review: an attached work title differs; check audio, versions and agreements before any correction.

How this review was built

Catalog discovery reconciled the complete Chartmetric artist-track CSV (including nested recording versions) with Deezer releases, contributor discovery and Spotify appearances. Each Chartmetric artist was matched by its exact Spotify artist URL. All retained recordings are shown without a track sample, including Spotify-confirmed entries whose ISRC was not supplied. Those entries remain marked for identifier and registry-link verification. Known same-name collisions remain in a separate identity-review ledger. Label-supplied writing credits are included where the source returns them.

MLC searches used recording-artist names, exact legal writer-name variants and available IPI evidence, followed by recording-identifier checks. Artist searches are relevance-ranked; broad Luh Nae and Yung Wolf searches were limited to the first 200 candidates, and the Bfb Da Packman artist query to the first 2,000 candidates. The complete exact legal-name searches and recording-identifier evidence drive classifications. Missing matches mean “no MLC work identified in this review,” not proof of universal non-registration.

Writer-proof headline counts require a legal writer-name or corroborated IPI match, or a stage-name credit corroborated by the catalog identity and credits. Same-name Yung Wolf writer entries remain unverified where the legal identity is not resolved. Label credits are shown separately. A claim is attributed only when a positive publishing/admin share has a writer chain naming that identity. Empty writer lists and unrelated co-writer claims do not count as attributable. Artist-named or independently client-associated publishers with empty writer lists are set aside for chain/control verification and excluded from the apparent-gap count. Distinct same-title registry records are grouped only when a reviewed recording connects them, then flagged for reconciliation in the composition audit. Identical titles on unconnected recordings are not merged.
