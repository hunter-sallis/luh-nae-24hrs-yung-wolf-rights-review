How to read these statuses
  • No MLC work identified: no matching work found in the scope reviewed; investigate before filing a new registration.
  • Registered · shares remain open: a work exists with less than 100% total known shares; the remainder is not the artist’s personal split.
  • Registered · 100% claimed: the work is fully claimed across listed interests; artist attribution still requires a separate review.
  • Writer attribution needs verification: a matching work exists, but the artist’s legal writer identity is not established on its writer line.
  • Work found · recording link unconfirmed: title and legal writer match an existing work, but the reviewed recording link was not confirmed.
  • Recording-to-work match needs review: an attached work title differs; check audio, versions and agreements before any correction.
  • Needs verification: a possible composition match or other unresolved evidence prevents a reliable classification.

Method & scope

How this review was built

Catalog discovery used Deezer artist releases plus a paginated track search, retaining only recordings whose contributor metadata names the artist profile. ISRCs were deduplicated. Label-supplied composer and author names were captured per recording. Yung Wolf’s same-name candidates were separated using label, legal-name credits and verified social/DSP releases.

MLC searches used recording-artist names, exact legal writer-name variants and available IPI evidence, followed by recording-identifier checks. Artist searches are relevance-ranked; broad Luh Nae and Yung Wolf searches were limited to the first 200 candidates. The complete exact legal-name searches and recording-identifier evidence drive classifications. Missing matches mean “no MLC work identified in this review,” not proof of universal non-registration.

Writer-proof headline counts require a legal writer-name match or a stage-name credit corroborated by the catalog identity and credits. Same-name Yung Wolf writer entries remain unverified where the legal identity is not resolved. Label credits are shown separately. A claim is attributed only when a positive publishing/admin share has a writer chain naming that identity. Empty writer lists and unrelated co-writer claims do not count as attributable. Artist-named publisher entities with empty writer lists are set aside for control verification and excluded from the apparent-gap count. Distinct same-title registry records are grouped only when a reviewed recording connects them, then flagged for reconciliation in the composition audit. Identical titles on unconnected recordings are not merged.

Spotify counts were read in the browser for the ten Popular-table recordings per artist. Version-ambiguous counts appear only in the exposure table. Other counts remain unmeasured. BMI/Songview account and repertoire verification were not completed in this review; no conclusion is drawn about PRO registration or actual payment. GMR was not reviewed; the sampled exposure does not establish a full-catalog 100-million-play threshold.

New, removed, SoundCloud-only or uncredited producer placements may sit outside the reviewed set. Data reflects the public sources on 2026-11-02 and can change.
